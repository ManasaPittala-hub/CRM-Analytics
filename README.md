# CRM Analytics – Lead & Opportunity

## 📌 Project Overview

CRM Analytics project focused on analyzing **Lead and Opportunity data** to understand lead conversion, sales pipeline performance, opportunity performance, revenue, and key business KPIs.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow, from **data cleaning and transformation to SQL analysis and interactive dashboard development** using Excel, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Clean and prepare Lead and Opportunity datasets
* Perform data analysis and KPI calculations
* Analyze lead conversion and opportunity performance
* Validate business metrics using SQL
* Build interactive dashboards for business reporting
* Present meaningful insights through data visualization

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Excel** – Data cleaning, transformation, analysis, KPI calculations, and dashboards
* **SQL** – Data querying, analysis, KPI calculations, and data validation
* **Tableau** – Interactive Lead and Opportunity dashboards
* **Power BI** – Interactive Lead and Opportunity dashboards

## 🔄 Project Process

### 1. Data Preparation – Excel

Lead and Opportunity data were cleaned, transformed, formatted, and validated. Separate Excel dashboards were developed to analyze the two datasets.

### 2. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to perform data analysis, aggregations, KPI calculations, and validation of Lead and Opportunity metrics.

### 3. Tableau Dashboards

Interactive dashboards were created for both Lead and Opportunity data to visualize KPIs, trends, sales pipeline performance, and opportunity analysis.

### 4. Power BI Dashboards

Power BI was used to develop separate Lead and Opportunity dashboards with interactive visualizations and KPI-based business reporting.

## 📊 Key KPIs

The Opportunity analysis includes KPIs such as:

* Expected Amount – **$184M**
* Total Opportunities – **2,646**
* Conversion Rate – **31%**
* Win Rate – **43%**
* Loss Rate – **42%**

Additional Lead and Opportunity KPIs are included within the respective dashboards.

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

* Lead conversion and performance
* Opportunity pipeline analysis
* Expected revenue and opportunity value
* Active and closed opportunities
* Closed Won analysis
* Win and loss performance
* Opportunity trends by fiscal year
* Opportunity analysis by industry and opportunity type
